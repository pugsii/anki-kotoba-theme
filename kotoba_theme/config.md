Kotoba Theme colours all of Anki with one of its themes, in light or dark (pick them from the cog at the
top right of the Decks screen).

- `theme`: the theme, when the Kotoba note type isn't installed. With it, the theme is kept in the note type's
  Styling instead, so your cards on every device use it too.
- `storage`: the cards' saved ⚙ settings and image choices. Managed automatically.

Tools → *Kotoba: install or update note type* adds the Kotoba note type (or updates it, keeping your settings).
